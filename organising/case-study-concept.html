Research

Can AI Create New Revenue?

We surveyed the AI landscape across healthcare and generated multiple product directions. On paper, many of them looked commercially attractive.

After discussing with customers and industry leaders at a healthcare conference, we realised the market was already crowded — and the real opportunity was hiding inside our own company.

The reframe

From "What AI products could we build?" → "Which of our existing capabilities could AI make impossible to replicate?"

Opportunity

The Opportunity Was Already There

OpenLoop already handles the hard parts of telehealth — clinical operations, pharmacy workflows, compliance. Past customers were established enterprises who just needed the clinical layer.

But creators, influencers, and small wellness brands increasingly had audiences — but no way to build a telehealth business on top of it.

Use AI to turn OpenLoop's complex medical capabilities into something anyone can configure, launch, and operate.

Iteration

The Best AI Stays Out of the Way

In the demo, AI Chat made the product feel intelligent. But real onboarding is not a demo. Users are there to complete setup and launch — not to explore AI.

"Sometimes the best AI is the one that does not pull attention away from the task."

Principle 01
Making the launch process legible
Workflow-level IA so users always know where they are and what remains.
Principle 02
Rethinking how products are presented
From blank chat to AI recommendation summary. From data table to card grid.
Principle 03
Making brand generation iterative
Generate More and Refine with AI replaced destructive regeneration.
Principle 04
Seeing decisions before committing
Live storefront preview updated in real time as brand choices were made.
